CommunityOne's goal is one map for every community: residents, leaders and funders looking at the same record of what is happening on the ground. Three ways to help. Try it, and tell us where your community is missing or wrong. Contribute on GitHub, where every task is a logged issue. Or partner with us: data, funding, or a community that wants to pilot a readable public record. Thank you.
