Who it’s for

Four kinds of people, one record

WhoTheir questionWhat Open Navigator gives them
ResidentsWhat did my city or school board decide, and what is coming up?Search by place and topic; each decision with the quote from the record
Advocates and nonprofit leadersWhere is my issue moving, and what arguments are being made?Policy questions tracked across places, contested votes, the arguments for and against
Journalists and researchersWhat does the record say, across many places at once?Cited, downloadable data; open datasets; an API and an AI-assistant connection (MCP)
Funders and civic leadersWhere are needs and resources out of step?Budgets and nonprofit filings next to what officials said in meetings

2

Four audiences, and they ask different questions of the same public record. Residents want to know what was decided near them and what is coming up. Advocates and nonprofit leaders want to follow an issue across places and see the arguments. Journalists and researchers want the record itself, cited and downloadable, at scale. Funders and civic leaders want to see where money and stated priorities line up and where they don't. The rest of the deck shows how one platform serves all four without making anything up.
