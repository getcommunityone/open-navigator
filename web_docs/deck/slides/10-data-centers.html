This quarter, most of the work goes to data centers, because that's where residents are asking the hardest questions: what's being built near me, who approved it, what it will do to my power bill, and whether the promised jobs are real. We start with two of those. First, job promises on the record: when someone at a meeting promises jobs, we show construction jobs and permanent jobs separately, each with its dates, because a two-year build isn't a career. Every number says who said it, at which meeting, and links to that moment in the recording, and if a number was never stated we say so instead of showing zero. Second, the pipeline near me: proposed sites before the vote, matched to the agenda where they'll come up, with alerts ranked by local concern. To be clear, this is planned work for October through December; none of it is built yet, and it builds on the meeting citations planned in the core work.
